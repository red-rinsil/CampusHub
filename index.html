The problem we solve

Less chasing information.
More confidence in what comes next.

Important academic updates are often fragmented across group chats, email threads, paper notices, and separate calendars. That makes it easy to miss a room change, deadline, or exam announcement.

01

Schedules in one view

Check your weekly classes, instructors, rooms, and schedule changes without piecing together old messages.

02

Dates you can count on

See exams, quizzes, project deadlines, consultations, and special sessions together in chronological order.

03

Updates with follow-through

Professors and officers publish official notices. Students acknowledge them, so important messages don’t disappear into a chat.

Built for campus teams

Clear information helps everyone move together.

Students get a dependable place to check what’s next. Professors and class officers can share changes. Administrators can manage accounts. Special roles are granted only by redeeming a one-time access key.

CampusHub uses Supabase Authentication and a shared database. Account roles, one-time keys, and data access are enforced by database policies.

Role access

Redeem an access key

A valid one-time key upgrades your account to Professor, Class Officer, or Administrator. Ask your campus administrator for a key.

Ask a CampusHub administrator for a one-time key. Keys are generated and redeemed by the shared database and cannot be reused.

Student section access

Join your section

Enter the reusable key shared by your class officer or administrator. You can belong to one section at a time. Joining gives access to the subjects in that section.

Bulk data tools

Import schedules & exams

Upload a CSV or .txt file with one class or exam per row. Valid rows are previewed before they are added to your timetable and calendar.

CSV columns

Type, Subject, Professor, Classroom, DayOfWeek, Time, EndTime, Date, Title, Details

Classes need Subject, an assigned Professor, Classroom, DayOfWeek, Time and EndTime. Exams/quizzes need Type, Subject, Classroom, Time and a real Date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
